# Week 1 — Category Rules (v1)

Written before labelling any data.

## Sports
Matches, results, athletes, teams, tournaments.
- Mainly about money or ownership in sport → Business
- Mainly about politics or security around sport → World
- Mainly about technology or research in sport → Sci/Tech

## Business
Companies, markets, earnings, the economy, trade, jobs, deals.
- Mainly about a match or tournament (even if sponsored or held by a business) → Sports
- Mainly about politics or conflict affecting business → World
- Mainly about technology or research inside a company → Sci/Tech

## World
International news, politics, conflict, disasters, national affairs, diplomacy.
- Mainly about athletes or tournaments → Sports
- Mainly about companies or the economy → Business
- Mainly about technology or research → Sci/Tech

## Sci/Tech
Science, research, technology, software, space, health research.
- Mainly about money or ownership of tech (earnings, share price, acquisitions) → Business
- Mainly about politics or security around tech → World
- Mainly about matches or tournaments involving tech → Sports

## Tie-break
If two rules point at each other, label by what the headline is mainly about.
The first sentence decides.
